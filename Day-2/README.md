# 📊 Northwind Database Analysis using SQL & Pandas

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Northwind sample database using SQL and Python to answer real-world business questions. SQL queries were used to extract meaningful business information, and the results were imported into Pandas for exploratory data analysis (EDA) and visualization.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate the integration of SQL and Python for business intelligence and data-driven decision-making.

---

# 🗄️ Database Overview

The **Northwind** database is a sample relational database that represents the operations of a trading company. It contains information about:

- Customers
- Orders
- Order Details
- Products
- Categories
- Suppliers
- Employees
- Shippers

The database enables the analysis of sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, and revenue trends.

---

# ❓ Business Questions

The following business questions were addressed using SQL:

1. Find the Top 10 Selling Products.
2. Identify the Top 10 Customers by Revenue.
3. Analyze Monthly Sales Trends.
4. Discover the Best Performing Product Categories.
5. Calculate Customer Purchase Frequency.

# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

---

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Top-selling products contribute significantly to the company's overall sales, indicating strong customer demand for a limited number of products.

### 2. A small group of customers generates a substantial portion of the total revenue, highlighting the importance of customer retention and relationship management.

### 3. Monthly sales exhibit noticeable fluctuations, suggesting seasonal variations and changing customer purchasing patterns.

### 4. Certain product categories consistently outperform others in terms of revenue, making them valuable areas for future investment and inventory planning.

### 5. Customer purchasing frequency varies considerably, with a few customers placing orders much more frequently than the average customer, indicating opportunities for customer segmentation and loyalty programs.
